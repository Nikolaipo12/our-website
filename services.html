Barangay services

Available services for residents and document requests, with straightforward instructions.

Available barangay services

Document

Barangay Clearance

Proof of residency for employment, school, or official applications.

Processing
1–2 working days

Bring: valid ID and proof of address.

Request this service
Assistance

Certificate of Indigency

Support for medical assistance, scholarships, and social services.

Processing
2–4 working days

Bring: valid ID and income documents.

Request this service
Business

Business Permit

Clearance for new and renewing businesses operating in the barangay.

Processing
2–3 working days

Bring: DTI registration, lease or ownership proof, and valid ID.

Request this service
Resident ID

Community ID

A barangay-issued ID for residents who need local identification.

Processing
Same day

Bring: valid ID, 1×1 photo, and proof of residency.

Request this service

Processing times may change. Please confirm with the barangay office before filing.

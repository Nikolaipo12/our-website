Barangay services

Available services for residents and document requests, with straightforward instructions.

Clearance

Barangay Clearance

For employment, school, and residency verification.

Submit a request for a barangay clearance to confirm your residence within Barangay Sta. Lucia.

Requirements: ID, proof of address
Processing: 1–2 working days
Certificate

Certificate of Residency

Confirm residency for permits and applications.

A formal certification that you reside in Barangay Sta. Lucia.

Requirements: ID, barangay certification form
Processing: 1–3 working days
Certificate

Certificate of Indigency

Needed for social assistance and scholarships.

Request an indigency certificate with supporting documents from the barangay office.

Requirements: ID, income documents
Processing: 2–4 working days
Documents

Other Barangay Documents

Request certificates, affidavits, or community endorsements.

Submit a general service request for documentation support from the barangay office.

Requirements: ID, supporting documents
Processing: 1–5 working days

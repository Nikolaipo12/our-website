About the barangay

Barangay Sta. Lucia

Our history, mission, vision, and community priorities.

Barangay history

Barangay Sta. Lucia has served its community since 1985, growing as a safe and supportive neighborhood filled with local programs and resident participation.

Mission & vision

Mission: To provide inclusive, transparent, and responsive services for every resident.
Vision: A thriving barangay where everyone feels safe, heard, and supported.

Goals

Strengthen community services, improve public safety, expand health and education support, and foster active resident participation.

Location

Barangay Sta. Lucia is located in the heart of Santa Ana, Pampanga, with easy access to public transport, schools, and the barangay hall.
